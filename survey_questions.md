## Section 2 — School Ostracism Index (SOI), modeled on OES-A

Scale: 1 = Never, 2 = Rarely, 3 = Sometimes, 4 = Often, 5 = Almost always.
Reference period: past school year.

- **Q5.** Classmates deliberately didn't invite me to join group projects or activities.
- **Q6.** I was left out when the class split into groups or cliques.
- **Q7.** My messages in group chats were ignored or went unanswered.
- **Q8.** I felt like classmates "looked through me" as if I wasn't there.
- **Q9.** People stopped including me in things they invited everyone else to.

## Section 3 — Goal Abandonment Ideation (GAI), modeled on Wrosch et al. goal-disengagement scale

Scale: 1 = Never, 2 = Once, 3 = 2–3 times, 4 = 4–6 times, 5 = More than 6 times.
Reference period: past 3 months.

- **Q10.** I seriously considered quitting my project or giving up on my main goal.
- **Q11.** I wanted to drop everything just to feel "normal" like everyone else.
- **Q12.** I thought my goal wasn't worth the social price I pay for it.
- **Q13.** I thought about lowering my ambitions just to be better accepted by peers.

## Section 4 — Community Belonging (CF-belonging), modeled on SDT relatedness subscale

Filter: *"Do you have an online or offline community of like-minded peers?"* Yes/No. If No → skip block.

Scale: 1 = Strongly disagree … 7 = Strongly agree.

- **Q14.** In this community, I feel like I'm "among my own people."
- **Q15.** There are people here who understand what I do and why.
- **Q16.** My ambitions are seen as normal here, not strange.
- **Q17.** I feel supported when I share difficulties with my project.

## Section 5 — School Burnout (single-item, modeled on SBI exhaustion subscale)

Scale: 1 = Strongly disagree … 6 = Strongly agree.

- **Q18.** I feel worn out because of my schoolwork.

## Section 6 — PHQ-2 (Kroenke, Spitzer & Williams, 2003)

Scale: 0 = Not at all, 1 = Several days, 2 = More than half the days, 3 = Nearly every day.
Reference period: past 2 weeks.

- **Q19.** Little interest or pleasure in doing things.
- **Q20.** Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless.

## Section 7 — Optional qualitative

- **Q21.** *(Optional, 2–3 sentences max)* Describe a moment when your school environment's reaction to your project or ambitions affected your motivation to keep going.
